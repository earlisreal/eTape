# Fractional stock orders

Status: Approved

## Goal

Trade fractional long positions in US stocks on Alpaca Paper and Live through the Order Ticket, Chart Risk Entry, Chart Order Gestures, hotkeys, and Deck Buttons. Show fractional eligibility for the active market session, focused symbol, and selected Execution Venue in the Top Bar.

## Confirmed behavior

- Put an `F` tile after `T`, `M`, and `S`. Use green for eligible and a slash for ineligible in the active session. Use a muted tile when eligibility is unknown and during closed or overnight phases. The hover tooltip and accessible label show the active session, its eligibility, and both Alpaca values, `fractionable` and `fractional_eh_enabled`, as true, false, or unknown. Omit the tile when the selected venue lacks the base `fractionable` field. Use the same venue and symbol keys as the other tiles.
- A fractional Alpaca BUY opens or adds to a long on a tradable, fractionable stock. A fractional SELL reduces a confirmed long position at its original Execution Venue. Cap the sell at the available quantity after working exits. [Alpaca fractional trading notes](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/fractional-trading#notes-on-fractional-trading)
- Stock SHORT and COVER use whole shares across all entry surfaces. Chart Risk Entry Auto resolves the direction first and applies whole-share sizing to a short and its protective COVER. `F` reports fractional long eligibility alongside the independent `S` classification for short selling.
- Dollar, cash, buying-power, applicable position percentages, and chart risk budgets resolve to fractional shares by default on eligible Alpaca orders. For example, $550 at $400 per share resolves to 1.375 shares for an eligible order. Calculated orders on other routes size in whole shares. An explicit fraction that the route cannot accept returns a reason. Alpaca documents up to nine decimal places for share quantity. Verify the applicable order minimum and the handling of small closing balances during broker testing. [Alpaca fractional trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/fractional-trading)
- Use Shares mode for an exact whole-share quantity in the workflows that offer it.
- Cancel and re-entry are separate trader actions. Enable price-only changes on a working fractional order if Alpaca Paper accepts the PATCH with `qty` omitted. Apply ordinary edit validation to engine-held instructions before their broker order exists. [Alpaca replace-order reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/patchorderbyorderid-1)
- Position % sizes an eligible sell from the latest confirmed position at its Execution Venue and rounds down to the supported share increment. Selling 50% of 0.75 shares submits 0.375 shares. The linked Chart Risk Entry stop reduces coverage as the sell fills, leaving 0.375 shares protected after a complete fill. Account for any working sell reservations before routing another exit.
- Display decimal quantities in orders, fills, positions, chart overlays, and Trade History. A 100% exit uses the full broker-confirmed position, including any fractional remainder. Test exact close and venue-wide Flatten for positions that predate this release and for assets whose eligibility cannot be confirmed.

## Fractional trading sessions

The first release covers regular hours and eligible pre-market and after-hours routes. Fractional orders use DAY.

| Active session | Top Bar `F` shows eligible when | Order handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular hours | `fractionable` is true | Use the native DAY order types for the selected Alpaca venue. |
| Pre-market and after-hours | `fractionable` and `fractional_eh_enabled` are both true | Use a DAY limit. Chart Risk Entry and eligible conditional orders send a DAY limit when their eTape-held trigger fires. |

Alpaca identifies `fractional_eh_enabled` separately in its asset catalog. Treat an attribute absent from a supplied list as false and an unavailable list as unknown. Use the extended-hours value to determine `F` when `fractionable` is true and the active session is pre-market or after-hours. The order preview and engine evaluate the specific order, including its requested session and Tradable status. Alpaca's equity order matrix lists DAY market, limit, stop, and stop-limit, and the create-order reference describes fractional quantity for market DAY. Verify broker execution for the intended routes. [Alpaca fractional shares support](https://alpaca.markets/support/fractional-shares), [asset attributes](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/get-v2-assets-1), [order matrix](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/orders-at-alpaca#order-types-vs-supported-time-in-force), [create-order reference](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/postorder)

For a stock eligible for fractions only during regular hours, Chart Risk Entry automatically uses a regular-hours fractional route for new longs during that session. The entry and linked stop expire at the scheduled regular close, including early closes. The preview shows that deadline. Any remaining position stays visible with its protection status. Further fractional exits use the next eligible session. A calculated opening order outside regular hours sizes in whole shares if its route allows an order. Adds inherit the existing shared stop's session and deadline and size within those boundaries. [Chart Risk Entry adds](../../.scratch/chart-risk-position-adds/spec.md)

The engine resolves eligibility for orders at the selected venue.
2. Add a stock quantity policy at engine admission, including broker capability, asset eligibility, side, precision, order type, time in force, and session. Apply it to direct orders and again when held instructions route a child. Keep TradeZero's integer conversion behind whole-share validation. Serialize the submitted quantity as a canonical decimal. Alpaca's `extended_hours` flag also allows overnight execution. Check the allowed phase at dispatch, and verify broker DAY expiry at the 8 p.m. ET trade-date boundary. [Alpaca 24/5 trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/245-trading-for-trading-api)
3. Reuse the existing quantity fields on orders, fills, and positions and the existing SQLite fill storage. Use share increments for stock quantity comparisons and arithmetic where fill deltas, sell reservations, and flat detection meet. Use the documented decimal grid for Alpaca while verifying the route-specific trading minimum separately. Computed sizes round down within their budget. Validate explicit input without silently truncating it.
4. Update [ticket sizing](../../ui/src/chrome/exec/sizing.ts), the [Order Ticket](../../ui/src/chrome/panels/OrderTicketPanel.tsx), and [Action Templates](../../ui/src/chrome/exec/actionTemplate.ts) used by hotkeys, Deck Buttons, and Chart Order Gestures. Resolve the actual route before calculating shares. Use a current side-appropriate quote for a market order and the effective limit for a limit order. Use the same policy for preview and submit. Existing and imported calculated recipes inherit fractional sizing where eligible. Document the new size behavior for existing dollar and risk recipes in the release notes. Exercise focus changes, template imports, and saved layouts with unrelated settings.
5. Update the [Chart Risk Entry preview](../../ui/src/chrome/panels/tv/ChartRiskEntry.tsx) and [engine sizing](../../engine/internal/exec/risk_entry.go). A fractional entry sizes within the risk budget, account funds, and reviewed quantity. Persist the reviewed sizing and session with the held entry so amendments and recovery use the same rules. Validate the entry and its protection before arming. If an admitted opening route loses eligibility, surface the reason and require review before routing a different quantity or session. Linked fixed or trailing exits follow actual fills, including a fractional partial fill from a whole-share order. Cover position adds, partial and late fills, and reduction alongside working exits. Apply the position-based quantity policy to deferred stops and standalone trails. Exercise restart recovery, including restoration of valid held orders and retirement of engine-run-bound trails.
6. Update [Account](../../ui/src/chrome/panels/AccountPanel.tsx) position-percentage sizing and per-position Flatten. Permit a position-backed reduction when opening eligibility is missing or false and surface the broker's result. Verify the [Alpaca venue-wide liquidation path](../../engine/internal/broker/alpaca/alpaca.go) and evaluate the per-symbol liquidation endpoint if the regular close route rejects an existing fractional balance. Check order/fill reconciliation, realized P&L, and Position Cycle detection. Add execution quantity formatting where the UI currently rounds to an integer. ## Acceptance and rollout

- In the browser, switch between Alpaca Paper, Alpaca Live, and another venue while changing symbols. Check that a late response cannot show the prior selection's flags. With `fractionable=true` and `fractional_eh_enabled=false`, verify `F` is green in regular hours and slashed in pre-market and after-hours. With both true, verify green in all three supported sessions. Check the transition at an early close, the muted state when eligibility is unavailable, and both raw values in the tooltip.
- Submit a fractional quantity from each supported entry surface using controlled data, and check the displayed size against the engine's accepted size. Exercise mixed whole and fractional partial fills, an exact 100% exit, concurrent closing requests, a smallest supported unit, and reconnect with an open fractional order or position. Assert that `0.1 + 0.2` shares bought and `0.3` sold reaches flat and closes the Position Cycle once.
- Fill a 0.75-share Chart Risk Entry, sell 50% via an existing Position % action, and reconcile partial and final sell fills. Verify that the final position and linked protection both show 0.375 shares. Flatten the remaining 0.375 shares and verify the position reaches flat and the linked protection retires.
- For a stock with both `F` and `S` true, verify fractional long sizing and whole-share short sizing with Auto direction. A short budget below one share produces no order. Reject explicit fractional SHORT and COVER orders. A SELL of 0.8 shares against 0.75 available is rejected, while 0.75 can close the position.
- On a stock eligible for fractions only in regular hours, preview and arm a fractional Chart Risk Entry during that session. Exercise a partial fill and verify that the remaining entry and linked protection end at the scheduled close, including an early close. Verify that a whole-share add inherits the longer session of an existing extended-hours shared stop.
- Verify broker payloads, accepted and rejected orders, cancel and supported price edits, trade updates, and restarted snapshots in fixtures. Verify actual DAY orders, protective exits, and small balance closes in account testing. Check actual pre-market and after-hours fills plus the broker's terminal status of a non-marketable DAY limit left through the 8 p.m. ET cutoff on an overnight-eligible symbol. Exercise delayed dispatch, early closes, and missed acknowledgements in controlled tests. Paper and Live use the same fractional routes. Live submissions use the existing venue arming, acknowledgements, and risk checks. Earl will test execution on Live.
- Run focused engine and UI tests, race checks for reservation and reconciliation changes, `gen-ts-check`, applicable lint/type/build checks, and browser workflows. Verify full hosted CI on the integrated `main` commit. Follow the [repository validation guide](../../AGENTS.md#validation).
- Before downgrading to a version that rounds execution quantities, resolve working fractional orders and confirm an exact close path for each outstanding fractional position.
