# Scanner

Rule 201 live triggers require the provider's latest-price update timestamp to be on the current ET date and at or after that day's regular-session open; chart/archive readers use temporary retryable caches where their provider data is incomplete.

Session-aware scanner polling, float/snapshot enrichment, filtering, and UI publication. Request/response scans consume no subscription slots; endpoint rate budgets still apply.

The board is sticky for one trading cycle: post-market movers accumulate with overnight, pre-market, and RTH movers. Entering post-market (normally 16:00 ET) clears the prior cycle. An engine started during RTH fetches pre-market once before merging the current RTH ranking; a failed bootstrap does not block RTH and is retried until successful. Changing filters or ranking mode clears the board and repeats that bootstrap during RTH. Every poll refreshes accumulated rows with one batched market snapshot. Premarket, after-hours, and overnight refresh from their matching session block and use its authoritative percentage; RTH calculates percentage from current price and last close. Missing session data or temporary snapshot failures retain the rank API values. REL VOL is eTape's Relative Volume (Daily Rate): live Moomoo cumulative volume from 04:00 ET divided by the arithmetic mean cumulative volume through the prior minute over the preceding 15 NYSE trading sessions. Its process-local worker fetches raw Alpaca SIP 1-minute history only for the sticky Scanner pool, capped at 30 symbols, and discards the raw bars after building a compact profile. Missing minutes inside a day contribute zero; an empty requested day leaves REL VOL unavailable, and an early-close day contributes only through its own data close. Request errors retry after 1, 5, 15, then 30 minutes; a complete profile or terminally incomplete response is cached for the ET day. Chart/archive history and `intraday_days` are not REL VOL inputs. A positive minimum REL VOL filters only new board admissions; pool warming ignores that threshold so history can load, and admitted rows remain sticky until the normal cycle/reset. The same 3203 snapshot also enriches each row with a derived/manual Rule 201 short-sell restriction estimate; the marker is informational and does not change scanner sorting, filtering, subscriptions, or order behavior.

Reported Short Interest is a board-only asynchronous 3249 enrichment. The worker requests one US symbol at a time, paces requests at least one second apart, keeps a process-local 24-hour cache, and preserves the provider's raw `sharesShort` plus `timestampStr` report date without split adjustment. Missing or failed refreshes stay unavailable or retain the last successful value; enrichment never blocks rank polling and successful results republish the complete `scanner.rank` payload.
