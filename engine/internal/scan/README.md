# Scanner

Rule 201 live triggers require the provider's latest-price update timestamp to be on the current ET date and at or after that day's regular-session open; chart/archive readers use temporary retryable caches where their provider data is incomplete.

Session-aware scanner polling, float/snapshot enrichment, filtering, and UI publication. Request/response scans consume no subscription slots; endpoint rate budgets still apply.

The board is sticky for one close-to-close cycle: post-market starts a new cycle, then its candidates accumulate with overnight, pre-market, and RTH candidates. At each stage, earlier successful rank sources are bootstrapped once per cycle while the current source is fetched continuously. Each rank direction requests up to 100 candidates; merged extended-session results are deduplicated and are not truncated to a 100-row board. Filter changes reset admission and alert qualification without deleting still-valid sampled history.

Every poll refreshes the sorted discovery/board universe with current-session snapshot fields. Premarket, after-hours, and overnight require the matching active snapshot block; RTH uses current price, volume, and `LastClosePrice`. A missing, stale, failed, omitted, or inactive-session observation is published as unavailable and never reuses the previous quote or seeds rolling history. The selected comparison is Previous close or a one-minute, five-minute, or one-hour sampled window. Rolling samples are taken at successful poll time, kept for one hour plus five seconds, and require continuity within five seconds; there is no interpolation or historical backfill for scanner percentages. `scanner.rank` carries `changeStatus`, the selected nullable `changePct`, and a per-symbol `alertSeq`; the UI treats revision advancement—not row arrival or `scanner.hit`—as the notification authority. Gainer/loser crossings use a 60-second per-symbol cooldown, while startup, filter, cycle, disconnect, and gap baselines stay silent.

The scheduler is serial and deadline-based with one-second fresh-install defaults for pre-market and RTH; slow work naturally produces a slower next start. Snapshot batches use a finite eight-request refresh budget and rotate the starting symbol when the universe exceeds that budget. OpenD Client protocol 3203 requests share a 550 ms minimum send-start gate across Scanner, Watchlist, and Stock Info; the gate covers this Client instance only, not another engine process or independently running SDK client. Symbol-specific application failures may be split within the finite budget; global, throttle, connection, permission-wide, and unknown failures terminate the refresh without guessing a bad symbol, then use a capped one-to-30-second retry backoff. Failed rank stages use their own capped backoff so a stale bootstrap retry cannot delay current-session discovery.

REL VOL is eTape's Relative Volume (Daily Rate): live Moomoo cumulative volume from 04:00 ET divided by the arithmetic mean cumulative volume through the prior minute over the preceding 15 NYSE trading sessions. Its process-local worker fetches raw Alpaca SIP 1-minute history only for the sticky Scanner pool, capped at 30 symbols, and discards the raw bars after building a compact profile. Missing minutes inside a day contribute zero; an empty requested day leaves REL VOL unavailable, and an early-close day contributes only through its own data close. Request errors retry after 1, 5, 15, then 30 minutes; a complete profile or terminally incomplete response is cached for the ET day. Chart/archive history and `intraday_days` are not REL VOL inputs. A positive minimum REL VOL filters only new board admissions; pool warming ignores that threshold so history can load, and admitted rows remain sticky until the normal cycle/reset. The same 3203 snapshot also enriches each row with a derived/manual Rule 201 short-sell restriction estimate; the marker is informational and does not change scanner sorting, filtering, subscriptions, or order behavior.

Reported Short Interest is a board-only asynchronous 3249 enrichment. The worker requests one US symbol at a time, paces requests at least one second apart, keeps a process-local 24-hour cache, and preserves the provider's raw `sharesShort` plus `timestampStr` report date without split adjustment. Missing or failed refreshes stay unavailable or retain the last successful value; enrichment never blocks rank polling and successful results republish the complete `scanner.rank` payload.
